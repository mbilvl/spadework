## Summary guidance for a website discovery pack

- Open the executive summary with what is being built and for whom, in one
  bullet. Follow it with the recommended stack and its yearly running cost
  from 06-tech-stack.md, then the build effort and the time to launch from
  09-roadmap.md.
- Give one bullet per brief priority saying how the pack serves it, or that
  it does not serve it yet and why.
- Take the key decisions from the decision log in 09-roadmap.md, keeping its
  wording, and add any decision from 06-tech-stack.md that the log misses.
- Order the consolidated open questions so that those blocking the launch
  phase come first, and say who can answer each one: the client or the
  developer.
- If 06-tech-stack.md or 09-roadmap.md is failed, invalid or pinned, say so in
  the executive summary, because the costs and the plan rest on them.
