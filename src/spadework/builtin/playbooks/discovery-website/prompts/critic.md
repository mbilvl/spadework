## Extra tests for a website discovery pack

Apply these on top of the general rubric. Raise each finding against the
document that should change.

**Consistency across documents** (kind `contradiction`):

- The site languages are the same in 01-scope.md, 02-audience.md,
  04-information-architecture.md, 06-tech-stack.md, 07-content-plan.md and
  08-seo.md.
- The recommendation in 06-tech-stack.md supports every content type, field,
  relationship and language in the content model of
  04-information-architecture.md, at the catalogue size the brief gives.
- Every page named in 07-content-plan.md and 08-seo.md exists in the sitemap
  of 04-information-architecture.md, under the same name.
- Costs and effort in 09-roadmap.md match 06-tech-stack.md and
  07-content-plan.md.
- 09-roadmap.md schedules every launch item in 01-scope.md, and nothing that
  01-scope.md puts out of scope.

**Priorities** (kind `priority`): each of the brief's priorities is served by
at least one concrete recommendation somewhere in the pack. If one is not,
raise the finding against the document best placed to serve it.

**Scope creep** (kind `other`): a recommendation for something 01-scope.md
rules out, or a feature that neither the brief nor an input asks for.

**Evidence** (kind `fabrication`): a named website, price, plan limit, legal
requirement or search term in 03-references.md, 06-tech-stack.md or
08-seo.md that does not come from that document's own sources.

**Client-readiness** (kind `other`): an open question the client could not
answer without a developer explaining it first.

**Money** (kind `other`): a cost with no currency, no period, or no
indication of whether it is one-off or recurring.
