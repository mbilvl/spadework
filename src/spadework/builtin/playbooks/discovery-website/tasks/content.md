This document lists everything the site needs before launch that is not
code: text, photographs, documents and translations. It is the client's to-do
list as much as the developer's, and content is what usually delays a
launch, so be specific and realistic about quantities and effort.

Base it on the page plans and the content model in
04-information-architecture.md, and on the photography direction in
05-design-direction.md. Use their names for pages, content types and fields.
Take what already exists (photos, texts, documents, translations) from the
brief and the inputs.

Write these sections as `##` headings, in this order, with exactly these names:

- **Asset checklist**: a table: the asset, the page or content type it is
  for, the quantity (from the brief or inputs, or "count needed"), whether it
  exists already (yes, partly, no, unknown), and the languages it is needed
  in. Include the text for the legal pages.
- **Shot list**: the photographs to take or commission, grouped (for example
  products, process, premises, people, finished projects), each with its
  purpose and the direction it follows. Say which existing photos could be
  reused and which must be replaced.
- **Copy templates**: a fill-in template for the two or three content types
  with the most entries, giving each field, a length and a note on tone.
  Write the template, not the copy: no product descriptions and no claims
  about the client.
- **Blog plan**: whether a blog or news section earns its place for this
  client, given the audiences and the time the editors have. If it does:
  four to six topics, each answering a real audience question, and a
  frequency the client can sustain. If it does not, say why in two lines.
- **Who provides what**: who produces each group of assets (the client, the
  developer, a photographer, a translator), with a range of effort where
  there is a basis for one. Leave dates to 09-roadmap.md.
- **Open questions**

Do not invent quantities. If the brief does not say how many items, photos
or documents there are, write "count needed" and ask for it.

Keep the document under 200 lines.
