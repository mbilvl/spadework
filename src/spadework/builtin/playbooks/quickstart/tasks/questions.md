The ten questions to ask the client next, ready to paste into an email or to
take into a call. The reader should be able to send them as they stand.

Work from the brief, the inputs and 01-landscape.md. Choose the questions
whose answers would change the most: the scope, the cost, the timing, or a
direction the landscape suggests. Leave out anything the brief or the inputs
already answer, and list it under Already known instead, so nobody asks the
client twice.

Write these sections as `##` headings, in this order, with exactly these names:

- **Questions**: exactly ten, numbered, most important first. Each item is
  the question in bold, in plain words the client can answer without help,
  followed on the same item by "Why:" and one short line on what the answer
  changes, and "Answer as:" with the kind of answer expected (yes or no, a
  choice, a number, a date, or a document they already have). When a
  question comes from something in 01-landscape.md, name the comparable.
- **Already known**: up to six bullets of facts the brief and inputs already
  settle, each saying where it comes from (the brief, or the input's
  filename).

In your result, put the six most important of the ten in the open questions.

Keep the document under 60 lines.
