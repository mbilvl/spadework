Usage: spadework playbooks [OPTIONS] COMMAND [ARGS]...

  List or show the built-in and local playbooks.

Options:
  --help  Show this message and exit.

Commands:
  list  List the built-in playbooks and the project's own.
  show  Show a playbook: what it needs from the brief, and its tasks in order.

  Examples:

    spadework playbooks list
    spadework playbooks show discovery-website
