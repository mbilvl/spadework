# Client call — 30 September 2026

Video call, 45 minutes. Acme Fabrication: the commercial director and the
office manager. Us: the developer.

## Catalogue

- Confirmed size: 184 standard products in 11 families.
- Most products come in two to six sizes (for example, a worktable at 1200,
  1500 and 1800 mm). They want one page per product with a table of sizes, not
  a separate page for each size.
- Every product needs a datasheet. Today the office makes them by hand in a
  word processor. Uploading PDFs is fine for launch; datasheets generated from
  the product data would be "nice later".
- The office manager would rather import the products from the production
  spreadsheet than type 184 products in by hand. She finds the current site's
  editor clumsy but is comfortable with spreadsheets.
