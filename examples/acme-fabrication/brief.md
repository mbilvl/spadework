# The client

Acme Fabrication is a family-owned stainless-steel fabricator with 25 staff and
one workshop in an industrial park in northern Portugal. It makes equipment for
commercial kitchens: worktables, sinks and wash stations, wall and floor
shelving, trolleys, extraction hoods, and made-to-measure pieces built to a
kitchen designer's drawings.

There are roughly 180 standard products in about a dozen families. Custom work
is around 40% of turnover. About 60% of sales are in Portugal and Spain; the
rest go to France and other EU countries through dealers. Most orders arrive
through kitchen designers and equipment dealers who specify Acme products in
their projects. Some restaurant groups and hotels buy directly.

# The project

The current website dates from 2014. It has five pages, is in Portuguese only,
and carries no product information. Instead, the office emails a 60-page PDF
catalogue that is updated once a year and is always out of date. Dealers ask
for datasheets and photos by email, and the office answers 15 to 20 of those
requests a week.

The client wants an online catalogue with specifications, dimensions,
downloadable datasheets and a way to request a quote. There is no online
selling: prices depend on the dealer and the project.

# Goals

- Replace the PDF catalogue as the reference that dealers and designers use.
- Cut the repeated email requests for datasheets and photos.
- Win more quote requests from outside Portugal and Spain, France first.
- Look like a serious manufacturer when a designer or buyer checks the company
  after meeting it at a trade fair.

# Constraints

- Budget: around €12,000 to €15,000 for the build, stated on the first call.
  Running costs should stay under €50 a month.
- Launch before the trade fair in May 2027, where the company exhibits every
  year.
- The office manager will maintain the catalogue for a few hours a week. There
  is no developer in-house.
- Portuguese and English at launch; Spanish and French are wanted later.
- Company email runs on the current domain and must keep working.

# What already exists

- A logo in vector format, and two brand colours used on the vans and the
  brochure. No brand guidelines.
- Product data in a spreadsheet kept by production: codes, names, dimensions
  and steel grade. Descriptions exist in Portuguese only.
- Photos: a professional shoot of about 40 products from 2024. The rest are
  phone photos taken in the workshop.
- ISO 9001 certification, plus material certificates and food-contact
  declarations for each product family, as PDFs.
- The domain is registered with a local hosting company, which also hosts the
  current site and the company email.

# Out of scope

- Online sales, published prices, and a dealer login, in the first phase at
  least.
- A connection to the production or stock system.

# Open points

- Whether dealers should see different information from end customers.
- Who writes and translates the product descriptions.
- Which custom projects can be shown, since some customers may not allow it.
